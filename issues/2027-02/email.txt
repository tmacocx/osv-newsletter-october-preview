Mon Feb 15 · First Hand Foundation grants: next deadline Mon Feb 15
  Grants for kids 18 and under with a medical need, within income limits (for example $65,000 with one child). They pay for future therapy, equipment, AAC, and travel for care. Due the 15th of every month.
  First Hand grants: https://firsthandfoundation.org/grants/

Tue Feb 16 · IEA applications projected to open Tue Feb 16
  Tennessee’s Individualized Education Account for 2027-28 needs an active IEP and a prior year in a Tennessee public school.
  IEA: https://www.tn.gov/education/iea.html

Thu Mar 11 · Looking ahead: no school Thu Mar 11, clocks spring forward Sun Mar 14
  Rutherford County and Murfreesboro City Schools are out Mar 11. If sleep is fragile, shift bedtime 10 to 15 minutes a night the week before the time change.

ONE QUESTION, ANSWERED
Send us yours: https://ourspecialvillagetn.com/contact

February's question: "How do I know if my child’s therapist is a good fit?"
Fit matters more than a long list of initials. A good match can explain what they are doing and why, and progress shows up outside the therapy room.

1. Ask what they do every week. What share of their caseload has needs like your child’s, and when do they refer out? Credentials are clues, not proof.
2. Watch how your child is treated. Green flags: your child’s communication, including AAC and “no,” is respected, and goals connect to real life.
3. Know you can switch. Repeated distress, loss of trust, or goals you did not agree to are valid reasons to ask for a plan review, a different clinician, or a second opinion.

Choosing the right therapist: https://ourspecialvillagetn.com/resources/choosing-a-therapist
Therapy styles: https://ourspecialvillagetn.com/resources/therapy-styles
Parent-to-parent guidance, not medical advice.
