DECEMBER IN OUR SPECIAL VILLAGE

December is full: school programs, parties, travel, and gatherings that can stretch every nervous system in the house, including yours.

ONE QUESTION, ANSWERED
Send us yours: https://ourspecialvillagetn.com/contact

December's question: "How do we get through a big holiday gathering without everyone melting down?"
You cannot make a loud, crowded day calm. You can plan for the moment it gets to be too much, for your child and for you.

1. Plan the exit before you go in. Know where the quiet room is, when you will leave, and leave margin before and after. Pack the supports you both use, not only your child’s.
2. Decide what matters ahead of time. Pick the one or two expectations that are essential and let the rest wait. Two acceptable options beat inventing new ones mid-meltdown.
3. Set up a handoff. Agree on a phrase with another adult, like “I am at yellow. Please take over now.” Needing a second grown-up is not failure.

Nervous system guide: https://ourspecialvillagetn.com/resources/when-your-nervous-system-is-full
Sensory processing: https://ourspecialvillagetn.com/resources/sensory-processing
Parent-to-parent guidance, not medical advice.
