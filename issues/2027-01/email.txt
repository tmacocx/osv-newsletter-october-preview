ONE QUESTION, ANSWERED
Send us yours: https://ourspecialvillagetn.com/contact

January's question: "How do I ask my child’s school for a special education evaluation?"
Schools rarely offer first. You have to ask, in writing, and you can do it any time of the school year.

1. Put the request in writing. Email the principal and copy the district special education office. Describe what you see and ask for a full initial evaluation. Our guide has a letter to copy.
2. Know the two answers. The district must propose an assessment plan or refuse in writing (Prior Written Notice). “Let’s try interventions first” is not one of them.
3. Track the clock. Your signed consent starts 60 calendar days to evaluate and decide eligibility. Put the dates in your phone; STEP TN can help: 800-280-7837.

IEP & 504 guide and letter: https://ourspecialvillagetn.com/resources/iep-504
Getting evaluated: https://ourspecialvillagetn.com/resources/getting-evaluated
Parent-to-parent guidance, not legal advice.
