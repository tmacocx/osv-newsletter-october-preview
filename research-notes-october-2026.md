## Parent Q — sources

- [TDOE Dispute Resolution Processes](https://www.tn.gov/education/legal-services/special-education-legal-services/legal-dispute-resolution-processes.html) — the state's own suggested escalation (teacher → principal → request IEP team meeting → district special education director → IDEAdisputeresolution@tn.gov) and the three formal options.
- [TDOE Administrative Complaint Manual (June 2024)](https://preprod.tn.gov/content/dam/tn/education/reports/Administrative_Complaint_Manual.pdf) — lists "Failing to implement IEP accommodations or services" as a complaint ground; 60-calendar-day resolution; no advocate or attorney needed.
- [TDOE Timelines in Special Education (June 2023)](https://www.tn.gov/content/dam/tn/education/legal/Timelines_in_Special_Education.pdf) — administrative complaint must be filed within one calendar year of the alleged violation; due process within two years.
- Free-help phone numbers (STEP TN 800-280-7837 / Español 800-975-2919; Disability Rights Tennessee 800-342-1660) match the OSV [IEP & 504 guide](https://ourspecialvillagetn.com/resources/iep-504).

## Design pass — what changed and why

- **Removed** the "What is inside" grid (duplicated the section headers), the draft banner and hidden draft preheader, the "Section one … six" eyebrows, the generic "Open the Village calendar / More events" CTAs, and the off-center white-bordered image in "Keep the village close."
- **Events and deadlines are now dated lists** inside one card each (date badge · title · one line of essentials · one verb link), which is the pattern strong family/nonprofit newsletters use: essentials in the email, link only to register or read more. The previous draft had three events crammed into a paragraph and one event per 150px card.
- **Linking:** heavy linking to the OSV site is normal and healthy for a nonprofit digest as long as (a) time-sensitive facts live in the email, (b) each link is a deep link to the actual registration or source page, and (c) links look like links. All links are now underlined and rust-colored; each item has one link (two only where two districts or two agencies apply); guide and Hope links point to OSV pages, event links point to organizers.
- **Info density:** body copy 15–16px, headings 28/24px mobile, ~10-line max per item, section spacing tightened from 44px to 38px. Mobile render at 390px checked for overflow (none) and for link wrapping (each link is no-wrap so lines break between links, not inside them).
- **One primary CTA** (Village Hall, gold button) plus the site button at the end; everything else is a text link.
