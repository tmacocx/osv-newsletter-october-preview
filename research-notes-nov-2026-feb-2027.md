## NOVEMBER 2026 issue

- **Sunday, Nov 1, 2026: Daylight saving time ends.** Clocks go back one hour at 2 a.m. This can upset sleep routines, so it is worth planning for. Source: https://www.nist.gov/pml/time-and-frequency-division/popular-links/daylight-saving-time-dst. Quote: "During 2026, daylight saving time is in effect from March 8 at 2 a.m. (local time) to November 1 at 2 a.m. (local time)."
- **Sunday, Nov 1, 2026: HealthCare.gov Open Enrollment for 2027 opens.** Families can shop for, renew or change Marketplace plans for 2027. Source: https://www.healthcare.gov/quick-guide/dates-and-deadlines/. Quote: "Starting November 1, you can apply for 2027 coverage or update your application with any changes you expect for the year ahead." / "November 1: Open Enrollment starts."
- **Monday, Nov 2, 2026: TN Promise application deadline for high school seniors (Class of 2027).** Seniors who want tuition-free community or technical college must finish the TN Promise application by this date. Source: https://www.tn.gov/thec/news/2026/9/2/college-application.html. Quote: "THEC encourages students to use the month to explore their options and complete the TN Promise application by November 2."
- **Tuesday, Nov 3, 2026: No school for students in either district (Election Day).**
  - RCS holds a teacher admin day. Quote: "Tuesday, Nov. 3: Teacher admin day/Election Day (No school for students)".
  - MCS holds parent-teacher conferences and sends report cards home. Quotes: "Tuesday, November 3-Election Day-Parent/Teacher Conferences (Admin DD#4) (Day Out for Students)"; Report Card Dates: "Tuesday, November 3 @ Parent Teacher Conferences".
- **Wednesday, Nov 4 to Friday, Nov 6, 2026: RCS progress reports go home.** Source: RCS. Quote: "Wednesday, Nov. 4 - Friday, Nov. 6: Progress reports".
- **Friday, Nov 6, 2026: ACT regular registration deadline and accommodations deadline for the Dec 12 test.** Students who need testing accommodations must register and have the school official submit the request in TAA by this date. Sources: https://www.act.org/content/act/en/products-and-services/the-act/registration/test-dates.html and https://www.act.org/content/act/en/products-and-services/the-act/registration/accommodations.html. Quotes: "December 12, 2026 … Regular Registration Deadline: November 6"; accommodations table: "December 12, 2026 | November 6 | Dec 12 - Dec 20, 2026".
- **Monday, Nov 23 to Friday, Nov 27, 2026: Thanksgiving Break.**
  - RCS schools are closed all week. Quote: "Monday, Nov. 23 - Friday, Nov. 27: Thanksgiving Break (Schools closed)".
  - MCS students are also out all week. Quotes: "Monday, November 23-Wednesday, November 25: Thanksgiving Break (Day Out for School-Based Personnel)"; "Thursday, November 26-Friday, November 27: Thanksgiving Break (Days Out for All)".
- **Sunday, Nov 29, 2026: ACT late registration deadline for the Dec 12 test.** Source: ACT test-dates page. Quote: "Late Registration Deadline: November 29".

## DECEMBER 2026 issue

- **Monday, Dec 7, 2026: Medicare Open Enrollment ends.** This matters for grandparents and caregivers on Medicare. It is also the last day to change Medicare Advantage or drug plans for Jan 1. Source: https://www.medicare.gov/health-drug-plans/open-enrollment. Quote: "Open Enrollment happens from October 15 – December 7"; "(the plan must get your enrollment request by December 7)".
- **Thursday, Dec 10, 2026: EFS Family Office Hours, 10 to 11 a.m. CT.** This is for families who already receive an Education Freedom Scholarship. Source: https://www.tn.gov/education/efs.html. Quote: "December 10, 2026: Family Office Hours (10-11 a.m. CT)".
- **Saturday, Dec 12, 2026: ACT national test date.** Source: ACT test-dates page. Quote: "December 12, 2026". The special (accommodated) testing window is "Dec 12 - Dec 20, 2026".
- **Tuesday, Dec 15, 2026: HealthCare.gov deadline for coverage starting Jan 1, 2027.** Source: https://www.healthcare.gov/quick-guide/dates-and-deadlines/. Quote: "December 15: Last day to enroll in or change plans for coverage to start January 1."
- **Friday, Dec 18, 2026: Short day in both districts, and the last day before winter break.**
  - RCS: Quote: "Friday, Dec. 18: Abbreviated day for students and teachers (2 hours); End 2nd Nine weeks".
  - MCS: Quote: "Friday, December 18: Student Half Day #2 (3 hours, 30 minutes)".
- **Monday, Dec 21, 2026 to Friday, Jan 1, 2027: Winter Break in both districts.**
  - RCS: "Monday, Dec. 21 - Friday, Jan. 1: Winter Break".
  - MCS: "Monday, December 21-Friday, January 1, 2027: Winter Break (Days Out for School-Based Personnel)".
- **Tuesday, Dec 22, 2026: ACT scores released for the Dec 12 test.** Source: ACT test-dates page. Quote: "Score Release: December 22".
- **Calendar year 2026: ABLE account contribution limit.** ABLE TN says contributions are capped per calendar year. Source: https://able.treasury.tn.gov/faqs. Quotes: "ABLE accounts allow up to $20,000 in contributions per calendar year." / "The annual contribution limit for any ABLE account is currently set at $20,000." The ABLE to Work rule allows extra contributions for employed beneficiaries: "allows eligible individuals to contribute above the annual contribution limit if certain criteria are met." The page does **not** name a Dec 31 cutoff. "Per calendar year" is the only wording on the page, so the newsletter should not state a hard Dec 31 deadline as coming from ABLE TN.

## Could not verify (do not print as fact)

- **2027 Social Security and SSI COLA and the 2027 SSI federal benefit rate.** These have not been announced yet. ssa.gov still shows the 2026 figure (2.8%).
- **The date the January 2027 SSI payment arrives (possibly Dec 31, 2026).** SSA's 2027 payment-schedule PDF could not be read clearly enough to confirm it.
- **2027 ABLE / IRS annual gift-tax exclusion amount.** The IRS has not posted it yet. Its table ends at 2026 ("$19,000"). Note that ABLE TN separately says the ABLE limit is "currently … $20,000".
- **Dec 31 as a hard ABLE contribution deadline.** ABLE TN's FAQ only says "per calendar year".
- **Education Freedom Scholarship (EFS) 2027-28 application window.** No dates have been posted on tn.gov/education/efs or the applications page. Last year TDOE announced the dates on Nov 20, 2025, so the dates may appear in time for the Dec or Jan issue.
- **TN Promise FAFSA deadline (Apr 1).** This was not found on an official page that could be read. tnachieves.org returned an error.
- **Disability Day on the Hill 2027.** tndisability.org/ddh still shows only the 2026 event as over.
- **Salvation Army Angel Tree Murfreesboro 2026.** The 2026 page confirms the eligibility rules: Rutherford or Cannon County, children 12 and under, and "Children can not turn 13 before the end of 2026". It also confirms the application is at saangeltree.org and gifts are picked up in December at an assigned time. However, no open or close dates for registration are posted.
- **Toys for Tots Rutherford County 2026.** No official local page with dates was found.
- **Katie Beckett / TennCare.** The official page has no statewide dates for late 2026 or early 2027. Renewals are handled case by case.
- **RCS main calendar web page (rcschools.net).** The page loads its content with scripts, so it could not be read. RCS dates above come from the district's own calendar file, revised June 3, 2026.
