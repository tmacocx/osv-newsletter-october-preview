One question, answered

A real parent question answered plainly

 

Question

How do I ask the school for a sensory break without sounding difficult?

Answer
Lead with the ask as support for learning. Name the hard moments and one proposed support (quiet corner, headphones, timed break). For wording ideas and next steps, see the Village IEP & 504 guide, and join Village Hall on Oct 10 with Mercedes Lawson / A.C.C.E.S.S.
