October's question: "What do I do if my child's IEP isn't being followed?"
An IEP is legally binding. The school has to deliver what is written in it.

1. Put it in writing. Email the case manager and copy the principal: name what is missing, quote the IEP, ask for a fix by a date, and request service logs.
2. Ask for an IEP team meeting in writing. If things stall, copy the district special education director. Bring notes and a friend.
3. Use free state help. File a TDOE administrative complaint, or call STEP TN at 800-280-7837.

IEP & 504 guide: https://ourspecialvillagetn.com/resources/iep-504
TDOE dispute options: https://www.tn.gov/education/legal-services/special-education-legal-services/legal-dispute-resolution-processes.html
Parent-to-parent guidance, not legal advice.
