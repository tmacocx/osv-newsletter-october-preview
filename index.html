October gets full fast. Fall break is the first week, conferences come right behind it, and the holiday season starts asking things of us before we are ready for any of it.

Deadline watch

School

Oct 5–9 · Fall break, Rutherford County and Murfreesboro City Schools

Both districts closed all week. RCS report cards Fri Oct 16, conferences Tue Oct 20; MCS conferences Tue Nov 3 (no school). Conference day is a good time to ask for IEP progress data.  RCS calendar →  ·  MCS calendar →

Fri Nov 6 · ACT: register and request accommodations for the Dec 12 test

Requests for extended time, breaks, or a separate room go through your school’s testing coordinator by this date; late registration does not extend it.  ACT accommodations →

 

Insurance

Oct 15 – Dec 7 · Medicare open enrollment

For adult family members on Medicare: the one window to compare or switch drug and Advantage plans for 2027. Free help from TN SHIP, 1-877-801-0044.  Medicare.gov →

Nov 1 – Jan 15 · HealthCare.gov open enrollment for 2027 plans

Enroll or change plans by Dec 15 for Jan 1 coverage. Children may qualify for TennCare or CoverKids any time of year.  Dates and deadlines →

Any time · Katie Beckett, TennCare for children with disabilities

Not based on parent income. Apply any time; Part B has a waiting list, so sooner is better.  Katie Beckett guide →

 

Money

Thu Oct 1 · FAFSA for 2027–28 opens

For seniors and college students, including those with IEPs or 504 plans. Free; Tennessee Promise students must file by April 1, 2027.  StudentAid.gov →

Wed Oct 14 · 2027 Social Security and SSI raise (COLA) announced

Expected the morning the September inflation report comes out. New amounts start with January payments.  SSA cost-of-living page →

Mon Nov 2 · Tennessee Promise application deadline, class of 2027

Tuition-free community college or TCAT for this year’s seniors. School counselors and tnAchieves can help.  Apply at CollegeforTN.org →

 

Community

Mon Oct 5 · Voter registration deadline for the Nov 3 election

Register or update your address at GoVoteTN.gov. Early voting Oct 14–29; Election Day Nov 3. Voters with a disability (or age 60+) can vote by mail if the county receives the request by Sat Oct 24.  GoVoteTN →

Sun Nov 1 · Clocks fall back one hour

Daylight saving time ends at 2:00 AM. If sleep is fragile at your house, shift bedtime 10 to 15 minutes a night the week before.  How DST works →

One question, answered

Our recurring column: one real question from a local parent, answered plainly, every issue. Send us your question →

 

October’s question

“What do I do if my child’s IEP isn’t being followed?”

Answer

An IEP is a legally binding plan: the school has to deliver every service and accommodation written in it, not just the convenient ones. When that is not happening, you have more options than most parents are told.

1

Put it in writing. Email the case manager and copy the principal: name the service or accommodation, quote what the IEP says, describe what is actually happening, and ask for a fix by a specific date. Ask for service logs and progress data too; you are entitled to them. Keep the sent copy.

2

Ask for an IEP team meeting. You can request one in writing at any time, not just at the annual review, and the school has to respond. If things stall, copy the district’s special education director. Bring your notes and a friend to take notes for you.

3

Use the state’s free complaint process. If it still is not fixed, file an administrative complaint with the Tennessee Department of Education. No lawyer, no cost. “Failing to implement IEP accommodations or services” is one of the state’s own listed examples. TDOE must issue a written decision within 60 calendar days, and you have one year from the problem to file. Mediation is also available.

4

Get free backup. STEP TN, Tennessee’s parent training center, will review the IEP with you and help you prepare: 800-280-7837 (Español 800-975-2919). For a district that will not follow its own plan, Disability Rights Tennessee: 800-342-1660.

Bring your own version of this question to Village Hall on Sat Oct 10. Mercedes Lawson of A.C.C.E.S.S. is covering the IEP process and taking parent questions live; pay what you can, including nothing.
